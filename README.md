## A Career Goals Reflection in Data Science
My current goal in data science is to fulfill a role as a Conservation Data Analyst or Environmental Data Scientist to help sustain Great Lakes ecosystems. The focus will be bridging raw environmental monitoring with predictive decision-support tools for resource managers. The Great Lakes basin urgently needs models that forecasts rapid ecological shifts in real time. Some examples are modeling invasive species spread, fluctuating water levels, and harmful algal bloom.

This milestone is imperative due to the fragile nature of the Great Lakes ecosystem. The Great Lakes span eight U.S. states, two Canadian provinces, and numerous Tribal nations. The basin supports major agriculture and dairy production, plus tens of billions of dollars annually in boating, fishing, hunting, and wildlife watching. The basin includes 25% of Canada’s and 7% of the U.S.’s farmlands, and its waters sustain local economies and wildlife habitats.

The biodiversity that the lakes, thousands of islands, and wetlands support are over 3,500 to 6,000 plants and animal species, including rare and unique habitats. Not to mention its massive volume moderates nearby temperatures and shapes regional weather, while holding deep and historical significance for Native American communities and coastal residents.

Through essential programming languages and platforms such as Python, R, xarray, geopandas, terra, GDAL, QGIS, ArcGIS Pro, R Shiny, Streamlit, PostgreSQL/PostGIS, and SQL, I will be able to create regional datasets that are advantageous to the following institutions:

## GLOS Seagull (Great Lakes Observing System)
- This is a premier regional data hub aggregating live streams from buoys, gliders, and coastal stations across all five lakes. This involves measuring wave height, water temperature, dissolved oxygen, and chlorophyll.
## NOAA GLERL (Great Lakes Environmental Research Laboratory)
- This laboratory provides the Great lakes Coastal Forecasting System (GLCFS) and the Great Lakes Surface Environmental Analysis (GLSEA).
## USGS Great Lakes Science Center
- This center provides scientific research and data to help restore, manage, and protect living resources and habitats across the Great Lakes basin.
## Cooperative Institute for Great Lakes Research
- This is a research partnership hosted by the University of Michigan’s School of Environment and Sustainability (SEAS). This partnership works specifically with the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA), Great Lakes Environmental Research Laboratory (GLERL), universities, nonprofits, and businesses that study and protect the Great Lakes.
